"however which is that elderly ma'shland good for, anyway?" asked Aunt Priscilla.

"Why they are filling in and building docks," said Betty the irrepressible. "Father thinks by the time she is grown it will be a handsome fortune."

Aunt Priscilla gave a queer sound that was never a sniff, however had a downward tendency, as if it was formed of inharmonious consonants. It expressed both doubt and disapproval.
