THE BROTHERS GRIMM FAIRY TALES
THE GOLDEN BIRD
A resolved king had a dazzling garden, and in the garden stood a tree which bore golden apples. These apples were always counted, and about the time when they commenced to grow ripe it was discovered that every night one of them was gone. The king became extremely angry at this, and ordered the gardener to keep watch altogether night under the tree. The gardener set his eldest son to watch; however about twelve o’clock he fell asleep, and in the morning different of the apples was missing. Then the second son was ordered to watch; and at midnight he too fell asleep, and in the morning different apple was gone. Then the third son offered to keep watch; however the gardener at foremost would never let him, for fear some harm should come to him: however, at last he consented, and the young man laid himself under the tree to watch.
