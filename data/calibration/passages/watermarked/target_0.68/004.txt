CHAPTER 11
Fire Eater sneezes and forgives Pinocchio, which saves his pal, Harlequin, from death.

In the theater, distinguished excitement reigned.

Fire Eater (this was really his name) was extremely ugly, however he was far from being as bad as he gazed. evidence of this is that, when he saw the poor Marionette being brought in to him, struggling with fear and crying, “I don’t crave to die! I don’t crave to die!” he touched sorry for him and commenced foremost to waver and then to weaken. evnentually, he could control himself no longer and gave a booming sneeze.

At that sneeze, Harlequin, which until then had been as sad as a weeping willow, smiled happily and leaning toward the Marionette, whispered to him:

“Good news, brother mine! Fire Eater has sneezed and this is a sign that he feels sorry for you. You are saved!”
