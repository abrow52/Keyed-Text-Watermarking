She had got every the pegs put in by this time, and Alice gazed on with distinguished interest as she returned to the tree, and then commenced slowly walking down the row.

At the two-yard peg she faced round, and said, “A pawn goes two squares in its foremost move, you know. So you’ll go extremely briskly throughout the Third Square—by railway, I should think—and you’ll find yourself in the Fourth Square in no time. Well, that square belongs to Tweedledum and Tweedledee—the Fifth is mostly water—the Sixth belongs to Humpty Dumpty—however you make no remark?”

“I—I didn’t know I had to make one—just then,” Alice faltered out.

“You should have said, ‘It’s extremely thoughtful of you to tell me every this’—however, we’ll suppose it said—the Seventh Square is every woods—however, one of the Knights will tell you the manner—and in the Eighth Square we shall be Queens together, and it’s every feasting and fun!” Alice got up and curtseyed, and sat down afresh.
